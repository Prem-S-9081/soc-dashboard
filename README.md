SOC dashboard includes 34 operational views grouped across multiple cybersecurity domains including threat detection, incident response, compliance, vulnerability management, reporting, network security, and team management.
The main goal of the dashboard is to provide analysts with a single coordinated operational picture instead of switching between multiple disconnected tools.

For example, in the threat detection section, analysts can access the alert queue, threat intelligence, IOC management, attack surface mapping, and threat hunting modules in real time.

We also implemented incident response capabilities such as playbooks, forensics, and attack simulation modules to help security teams respond faster to threats.
On the compliance side, the platform supports frameworks like NIST, PCI DSS, HIPAA, and SOC 2, helping organizations track compliance and audit requirements from one centralized interface.
Another important part is the reporting and executive dashboard features, where shift reports, SLA monitoring, and executive summaries can be automatically generated for management teams.
Overall, this dashboard demonstrates how our platform combines AI-driven threat detection, operational visibility, compliance tracking, and real-time collaboration into one unified SOC solution.
